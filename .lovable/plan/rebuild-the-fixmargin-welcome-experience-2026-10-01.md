# Rebuild the FixMargin welcome experience

## Goal
Turn `/welcome` into a substantial, product-led overview inspired by Autodesk’s presentation discipline, using FixMargin’s real capabilities and fictional Harbour Yard sample data.

## What will change
- Replace the sparse poster-style opening with the selected Architectural Technical Editorial direction: dark technical stage, confident typography, restrained blue accents, and a detailed multi-screen product composition.
- Add a compact secondary navigation for Overview, Workflow, Capabilities, Demo, and Presentations.
- Present the complete lifecycle from tender and award handoff through estimating, procurement, site delivery, commercial control, programme, forecasting, and final account.
- Build large alternating explanatory sections with realistic interface demonstrations for specifications and revisions, supplier comparison and call-offs, GRNs and site reporting, variations and invoice disputes, planner and progress, and margin forecasting.
- Add an obvious guided entry into the Harbour Yard sample project, clearly marked as sample data.
- Add a compact presentations/resources section covering role-based walkthroughs, commercial-control overview, and pricing navigation, using only routes that exist.
- Retain the role coverage, sign-up/pricing actions, legal company wording, standalone layout, and public access behaviour.

## Visual system
- Keep the existing FixMargin logo and semantic navy/blue palette.
- Use Inter with JetBrains Mono for a precise architectural-product tone.
- Prefer deep charcoal/navy product stages, broad white editorial bands, crisp borders, subtle grids, and restrained motion.
- Avoid copied Autodesk assets, invented claims, customer proof, prices, accounting-sync claims, and BoQ-only storytelling.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for overflow and readable product demonstrations.
- Confirm navigation, sample demo, pricing, sign-up, and sign-in links work.
- Confirm one page heading, section headings, metadata, standalone layout, and no browser-console or build errors.
